Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Retroflex consonants: T, Th, D, Dh, N, L, S → ट, ठ, ड, ढ, ण, ळ, ष
  • Nasal consonants: G → ङ, J → ञ
  • Palatal śa: z, ç, sh → श
  • Long vowels: aa, ii, uu or A, I, U → आ, ई, ऊ
  • Vowel diacritics: -r, -ri → ृ, ॄ; -l, -li → ॢ, ॣ
  • Anusvara & Candrabindu: M → ं, MM → ँ
  • Visarga: H → ः
  • Nukta: = → ़
  • Vedic accents: _ (anudātta), __ (udātta), ___ (svarita)
  • Danda: / or | → ।
  • Avagraha: ' → ऽ
  • Om symbol: OM → ॐ
  • Numbers: 0-9 → ०-९; ^0-^9 for superscript numbers
